# Skill Cards
## What is a Skill Card
**Skill Cards** are cards that allow you to have bonuses during the game applicable to one or more teams depending on what is specified in the rules of the aforementioned card.
The amount of **Skill Cards** drawn depends on the mode you are playing in.
### All Skill Cards
- ***Air Support***: Allows you to call in an air strike based on a D6 die roll
	- *1-2*  => **Aerial Reconnaissance**: Grants +2 accuracy for one turn to one squad.
	- *3-4*  => **Direct Fire**: You can attack "Infantry" type units and inflict 1 (D6 roll 1-3) or 2 (D6 roll 4-6) damage.
	- *5*     => **Dive Bomb**: You can cause significant damage to vehicles and infantry. Accuracy and damage vary based on a D6 roll: 1-3 inflicts 2, 4-5 inflicts 5, 6 automatically destroys a vehicle, leaving infantry outside it unharmed if the target is a vehicle, or eliminates the entire squad if there are no vehicles present.
	- *6*     => **Carpet Bombing**: the entire enemy team is affected by the attack, Infantry suffers 1, vehicles suffer 10, in this case a D6 is not rolled
