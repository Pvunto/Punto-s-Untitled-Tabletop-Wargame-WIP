# Overview

The AT Rifleman is a specialized Infantry class focused on engaging and disabling enemy armored units. It is most effective when supporting other Infantry classes and tanks by providing anti-tank capabilities where they are needed most. AT Riflemen are equipped with weapons designed to deal significant damage to armored targets, but their specialized role makes them less effective against groups of infantry. They can operate alongside other classes to protect the squad from enemy armor and create opportunities for advancing units.

##  SPECIAL RULES

- They can pass through doors and windows and climb over any low walls
- If close enough to an enemy vehicle (1.5cm / 0.6inch), they can place a charge on it to blow it up the following turn.
